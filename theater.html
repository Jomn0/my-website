My Home Theater

A look at my projector, audio setup, and everything in between.

The Setup

This is where I'll talk about my home theater setup, how I put it together, and what I use it for.

Equipment

Projector

Optoma HD142X

I got this projector for $200, PLUS a 150-inch screen off Marketplace. It's worth almost $1,000 new and around $500 used, I think. Great projector.

The bulb burnt out while I was at work and my brother freaked out. He thought he broke it lol.

Receiver

Yamaha RX-V375

This is one of my favorite finds. Got it for $60 on Marketplace and replaced my old receiver.

I originally ran stereo with a stereo CD player, while the AUX input was connected to the projector for audio.

It was funny because my family had to unplug the HDMI cable from the receiver's single HDMI port and swap it with whatever they wanted to use (Switch, media stick, Blu-ray player, etc.).

The RX-V375 sounds amazing for my setup.

Subwoofer

Dayton Audio CS1000

Got this back in June for $200, and it shakes my couch. Best 10-inch subwoofer I've ever owned. Also my first one.

When I took it out of the box, the RCA pin sleeve broke off inside the amp board on the back. Not very fun. I managed to get it out, but it scared me for a minute.

Speakers

My Surround Sound Setup

Front L/R:
KS-707 bookshelf speakers from a Kenwood VR-716 HTB.

Center:
RCA 40-5038. Picked this up at a thrift store for $4.99. Looks AMAZING and sounds super great.

Surround L/R:
Also KS-707 speakers. Apparently they share the same model name as my front speakers.

Subwoofer:
Dayton Audio CS1000 (listed above).
